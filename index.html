Calculation (USD account assumption): risk amount ÷ (stop distance in price × contract size). The table unit's price size converts handles/pips/points into price distance. Lot sizes are rounded DOWN using your minimum lot and lot step so estimated risk does not exceed the selected budget. Set your own minimum lot, lot step, contract size and price size. Only linear USD-quoted instruments are directly supported. Non-USD quote currencies, inverse crypto contracts and instruments requiring currency conversion are not supported by this formula unless you enter a verified USD-equivalent value per price unit. Spread, commission, slippage and swap are not included.
